The Ceremony

The pause before anyone speaks. An aisle, a place to gather, and a sense of anticipation in the air.

The Celebration

The moment a gathering finds its own rhythm. A table that draws people in and music that brings them to their feet.

The Details

The things we notice up close. A folded napkin, the shape of a stem, and the light held in a glass.

The Atmosphere

The character of a room shifts as the light falls. Texture and candlelight make the same space feel different as the evening unfolds.

The Moments

A hand reaches for another. Someone steps closer. The smallest moments can hold the feeling of an entire day.
