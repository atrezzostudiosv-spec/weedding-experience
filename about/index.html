OUR PHILOSOPHY

An eye for
the meaningful.

Considered ideas. Quiet elegance. Celebrations imagined around the feelings we never forget.

Begin with thefeeling.

Think of the moment just before a ceremony begins, or the way a room feels when someone you love walks into it. Those feelings offer a place to begin. The setting, the pace and the rituals can grow from what the celebration means to the people in it.

Give the detailsa purpose.

A table should make it easy to stay in conversation. Light should make a room feel more welcoming as the evening goes on. Flowers, textures and the space between them have a part to play. Each choice can serve the feeling of the gathering as well as its appearance.

Make room forthe unexpected.

A plan gives the day room to breathe. It considers the transitions people will notice and the ones they should barely feel. A pause can make space for a late arrival, a spontaneous toast or a few minutes alone together.

THE HUMAN SIDE

The people are the reason.

What stays with us is often a small thing. Someone reaching for a hand. A friend’s expression during a toast. The conversations that carry on after the last song. A celebration can make room for those moments by paying close attention to the people who will be there.

What would you like to remember?

Begin with a feeling, a memory or the people you want beside you. The rest can take shape from there.
